Debit card purchase authorization

The path from the store terminal to the approval or decline response. The card network routes the ISO 8583 message; the authorizer validates, queries fraud prevention and, depending on the score, reserves funds in the ledger or declines for suspicion of fraud. Only approved purchases trigger a notification.

Steps
1
The terminal sends the purchase; the card network routes ISO 8583 message 0100 to the authorizer
2
The authorizer validates card status, expiry and cryptogram without calling another service
3
The authorizer calls fraud prevention and waits for the score; timeout is 120 ms, with a fallback score of 0.5 if there is no response
4
The authorizer requests a funds hold; the ledger checks the balance and writes the hold and outbox row in the same transaction
5
With sufficient funds, response 0110 with code 00 (approved)
6
With insufficient funds, response 0110 with code 51 (insufficient funds); nothing is held
7
Score of 0.85 or higher: response 0110 with code 59 (suspected fraud), without querying the ledger
8
The card network responds to the terminal in every case
9
Only for approved purchases, the authorizer publishes an event to the notification queue without waiting for a response; the customer receives a push notification
